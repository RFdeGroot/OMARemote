## What's new in 0.6.0

- **Files over SFTP.** *Browse files* (`b` on an SSH connection, `ctrl+alt+b` in its session) opens the host's files in your own file manager, whichever it is: GVFS mounts `sftp://` with your ssh, and the files show up as an ordinary folder to copy and drag in and out of. *Unmount files* (`u`), and a question when the last session to a host ends with its files still mounted (Settings › SSH can also unmount or keep them).
- **RD Gateway with its own sign-in.** A gateway can now have its own domain and its own password, saved in your keyring, for a gateway that signs in as another user than the server. Passwords with a comma now reach the gateway intact; one FreeRDP's gateway list cannot take (a quote in it) is asked for when connecting.
- **`ctrl+alt+w`** disconnects the session you are in: its tab closes, or its own window, for RDP, VNC and SSH alike.
